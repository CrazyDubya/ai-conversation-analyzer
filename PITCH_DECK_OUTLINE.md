## Presentation Tips

### Delivery
1. **Start strong**: Hook in first 30 seconds
2. **Tell a story**: Problem → Solution → Opportunity
3. **Show confidence**: Know your numbers cold
4. **Handle objections**: Anticipate questions
5. **Close with urgency**: Limited spots, closing soon

### Common Questions to Prepare For

**Q: What if ChatGPT builds this?**
A: We're multi-platform and 18 months ahead. If they build basic analytics, it validates the market and we become their enterprise tier or acquisition target. We're also building network effects through benchmarking that create lock-in.

**Q: How do you acquire enterprise customers?**
A: Product-led growth creates qualified leads. Individual users at enterprises become champions. We layer on outbound sales and partner with consulting firms who implement AI governance solutions.

**Q: What's your defensibility?**
A: Four moats: (1) Network effects from benchmarking, (2) Data assets from aggregated insights, (3) Integration ecosystem creates switching costs, (4) 18-24 month technical lead.

**Q: Why does this need VC funding? Can't you bootstrap?**
A: We could, but the window is closing. Enterprise customers need us now due to compliance. Speed to market determines winner. VC capital accelerates our timeline by 18 months.

**Q: What's your customer acquisition strategy?**
A: Three-part flywheel: (1) Content marketing drives free signups, (2) Product value converts free to paid, (3) Enterprise champions pull us into large accounts. Proven CAC of $50 for self-serve.

**Q: How big can this get?**
A: Gong.io (sales conversation intelligence) is worth $7.2B with $200M ARR. We're solving a bigger problem (all AI conversations, not just sales) for a bigger market (100M+ AI users). Conservative target: $100M ARR, $1B valuation.

## Design Guidelines

### Visual Style
- **Color Palette**: Professional, tech-forward (blues, grays, accent color)
- **Typography**: Clean, modern sans-serif (Inter, Roboto, or similar)
- **Images**: High-quality screenshots, professional photography
- **Data Viz**: Clear, easy-to-read charts with minimal decoration
- **White Space**: Don't crowd slides - one idea per slide

### Do's
✅ Use large, readable fonts (>24pt for body text)
✅ Include page numbers
✅ Limit text to 3-5 bullets per slide
✅ Use visuals to support your story
✅ Keep consistent design throughout
✅ Include your logo on every slide (subtle, corner)

### Don'ts
❌ Don't read slides verbatim
❌ Don't use clip art or cheesy stock photos
❌ Don't overcomplicate charts
❌ Don't use more than 3 colors
❌ Don't include walls of text
